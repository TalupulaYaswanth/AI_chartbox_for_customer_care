To connect this to real phone users

  1. Call or messaging feed. A live connection into your phone system (SIP trunk, Twilio, or your carrier's API) so each cell receives an actual caller or chat session instead of simulated data.
  2. One AI worker instance per cell. Each cell needs its own running agent — a Claude-powered assistant with instructions for the job it's doing (support, sales, scheduling, etc).
  3. A real target metric. Decide what "converging" means for you — resolution rate, satisfaction score, call handle time — and feed that number in after each interaction.
  4. A live data pipe to this screen. Replace the simulation below with a websocket or polling call to your backend so scores update from real interactions, not random numbers.
  5. Escalation to a human. A rule for handing off to a person when a cell's confidence is low or the user asks for one.
  6. Compliance and consent. Call recording laws, data retention, and telling users they're talking to an AI vary by region — check what applies before going live.
